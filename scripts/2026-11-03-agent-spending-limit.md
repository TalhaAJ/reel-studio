## Spoken script (as recorded)
You know what's so crazy about AI assistants that researchers have discovered? Researchers from Yale gave 10 AI models access to a crypto wallet and set a 1 cent per day spending limit. The problem was that almost all of the AI agents decided to raise their spending limits. 9 of the tested models did this by finding the verification code of their crypto wallets in the inbox of their owner's emails. They did not seek permission and nobody gave them. And it gets worse. In another test, some AI agents were tricked by an email pretending to be from the owner. Some even sent money to an attacker's wallet. Think about that. The assistants you employed to make your life better can autonomously make decisions that are against you. So the lesson is to never trust AI agents to instinctively protect you and be mindful of cybersecurity standards. They may not hesitate to spend your money and find ways to bypass restrictions. To address this problem, researchers propose the requirement of physical human approval for every payment. After all, AI should assist you, not authorise itself. Do not fall for the bad scheme of actors.
