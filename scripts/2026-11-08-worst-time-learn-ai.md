## Script (as written by Talha)

Imagine you wake up one morning, check the news, and find out some new AI model has just dropped.
And apparently, it's automated 90% of an entire industry.
Now you're panicking, thinking, "Bloody hell, am I gonna be out of a job next?"
So you decide, you know what? I need to start learning AI.
You head over to YouTube, search "How to learn AI", and voilà. Thousands of videos, thousands of creators, all teaching you the same thing in completely different ways.
And somehow, after watching ten videos, you know even less than when you started.
So you think, alright, maybe TikTok or Instagram will help.
You come across your favourite tech creator, and he's telling you he's got this amazing roadmap with all the resources you need to learn AI.
All you've gotta do is comment "AI" and he'll send it straight to your DMs.
So you comment "AI". You get the message, click the link, and where does it take you?
Straight to his £50 course.
You check your bank account, look back at the course, and think, "Yeah, maybe AI isn't for me."
And just like that, your entire AI learning journey is over before it's even started.
