## The script
Say every line **in full** on each take. If you fluff a line, pause, then restart the whole line.
`[brackets]` are hand and body cues.

**HOOK**
> You can now clone your own voice for free, on your own laptop, no ElevenLabs, no subscription.
`[point at the camera on "your own voice", then wave "no… no…" away with one hand]`

**NAME + PROOF**
> It's called VoiceStudio, it's completely open source, and it just blew up on GitHub with over ninety thousand stars.
`[hands apart wide on "blew up"]`

**FIRST**
> First, you give it a short clip of your voice and it clones it, right on your computer, nothing gets uploaded.
`[hold up one finger; tap your chest on "your voice"]`

**THEN**
> Then you type anything you want, and it reads it back in your voice.
`[two fingers; mime typing]`

**🔊 PROOF #1** (your screen recording, about 3 s)
> I cloned mine, Manchester accent and all. Listen.
`[lean in, point down at the screen card]`
→ **SCREEN:** you type *"This isn't me talking. This is my AI clone."* and press generate, and **your cloned voice plays it**. The music drops out here so it's heard clearly.

**AND FINALLY**
> And finally, it can dub your videos into six hundred and forty six languages.
`[three fingers; big open hands on "six hundred and forty six"]`

**🔊 PROOF #2** (your screen recording, about 2 s)
→ **SCREEN:** the dubbing screen. You pick Japanese, and the clone says *"Let's go, boys"* in Japanese. Keep this clip; it's used again at the end.

**PLUS**
> Plus there's no account and no API key, and it runs on Windows, Mac and Linux.
`[count off on fingers: Windows, Mac, Linux]`

**VERDICT** (your own words)
> My verdict? This is sick, and it's free, so it's worth trying. And if you don't like it, nothing comes out of your pocket.
`[lean into the camera on "My verdict?", short pause, then deliver]`

**CTA**
> So if you want the link, just comment voice down below and I'll send it to you directly.

**SIGN-OFF**
> Let's go, boys.
`[big energy: a fist pump or clap]`
→ **SCREEN/AUDIO:** straight after, your AI clone answers *"Let's go, boys"* in Japanese (the PROOF #2 clip), with the sting on top. End.

## Filming checklist
- Green screen, chest up, centred. **Leave room either side for your hands**, so they don't go off frame.
- Do 2–3 full takes of each line, with about 0.5 s of silence between attempts.
- Record "Let's go, boys" 3 times with different energy, and I'll pick the best one.
- Drop the file in `raw-footage/`.
