## Scoring rules (0–3 each, total /15)
1. **Heat**: is it blowing up *right now*? Score **velocity** (views per hour since posted); last 24 h counts most, 72 h max. Views/likes/reposts on X, quote-tweet storm, trending tab, several big accounts on it.
2. **His lane**: fits one of the channel lanes above (tools, models, true vs BS, drama, future advice, psychology/self-improvement) and can be explained simply to a beginner.
3. **Hook**: is there a one-line, sayable, slightly shocking hook with a number or a name in it?
4. **Showable**: tweets, headlines, demo clips or a live site Claude can capture.
5. **Take**: is there an obvious question he can have an opinion on (drama, "is this worth it", "is this BS")?

Bonus +2: a new chapter in a story he already covered (see the table).
Bonus +2 (learned 2026-11-22 watch): an AI model's answer that's going viral (Grok / Gemini / Claude replies). Bonus +1: bookmarks ≥ ~60% of likes (people save it, so it's useful). Bonus +1: a huge claim he can fact-check in simple terms.
Bonus +2 (learned 2nd watch): it fits one of his formats: explainer ("what is X and why it matters"), summarise-a-long-video, "testing X so you don't have to", build-to-flex, or insider sauce. Bonus +1: from a source he keeps stopping on (@rileybrown, @gregisenberg).
**Always write 3 hook options** per topic: he asked for a better hook on 3 notes.
Penalty: −3 already covered with no new chapter; −2 needs a paid account to demo; −2 only rumours with no credible source; skip entirely anything that's just one anonymous account's claim about a private person.

## Avoid
- Repeating a topic in `scripts/` without a genuinely new development.
- Engagement bait with nothing behind it ("AI will replace X in 6 months" threads with no source).
- Reposting another creator's video as the content. Their post can be the *source*, credited on screen (like @mattshumer_), not the reel.
