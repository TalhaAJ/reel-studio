**So the scout must:**
1. Never assume a like means agreement. Tag liked reels as **agree / useful** or **call-out**, using the comments.
2. Treat **"absurd claim + top comments already calling it out"** as a **reaction / true-vs-BS candidate**: get-rich claims, fake income, "cheat codes", sponsored hustle posts. Report the claim, the 2–3 most-liked call-out comments (they're the proof the audience agrees), and any **#ad / #partner / paid-partnership** tag (who's paying for the claim).
3. Look for **campaigns**: several creators pushing the same claim under the same sponsor tag (e.g. #everygenpartner) is a bigger story than one reel.

- **2026-11-23 · "Really good information I can twist into my own words and post."** Some reels are **remake material**: solid information he'd explain in his own voice, with his own take and examples. Example: @lostandlucky, "The less agents you build, the more money you will make" (whiteboard explainer: context, data…; caption "Agents are most definitely a waste of time for pretty much everyone to be building themselves"). 2.9k likes, 139 comments, **1,336 shares** (a high share-to-like ratio, so people send it to friends). He liked it.

**So the scout must also:**
4. Flag **remake candidates**: info-dense reels (explainers, frameworks, whiteboards, "the truth about X") with a **high shares-to-likes ratio** (≥30%) or a long watch/replay from him. Report the core idea in 1–2 lines and **an angle that makes it his**: his engineer experience, a UK/beginner framing, a counter-take, or a live demo. Never a transcript to copy; credit the original creator on screen if the idea is clearly theirs.

## The scout's routine (copying his)
1. **Hook test, 3 s:** read the title box / first caption and screenshot frame 1. Keep it only if it's in a lane (`analysis/taste-profile.md`) or the counts are high for its age.
2. **Watch 15–30 s** (human pace), then **open the comments** and read the top ~15 for 5–10 s. Scroll further only if there's an argument or a call-out.
3. **Classify:** useful / call-out (absurd claim + comments already calling it out, any sponsor tag) / remake (info-dense, shares ≥30% of likes).
4. **For in-lane creators:** open the profile → `__igs.profile()` (median, outliers) → open their top 1–2 outliers → read those comments.
5. **Don't replay** (he doesn't), and keep the pace human: 2–6 s between actions, ≤40 reels and ≤8 profiles per run.
