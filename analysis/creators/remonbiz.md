# Reference creator: @remonbiz (studied 23 Nov 2026)

**Use:** structure, story mechanics and idea types only. Never copy his identity, look or phrases. Screenshots in this folder.

**Account:** 410k followers, 396 posts, a typical reel **~263k views** (median of the last 35), top reels **2–3M**. Bio: "conquering life & writing code · ML Engineer · Ex Tik-Tok · tech, crypto & security". Lives in Japan (some reels are life vlogs there).

## 1. His story
- **A credentialed insider:** "22yo engineer" in every title, ML engineer, ex-TikTok. The age plus the job is the whole persona: young, but he knows things you don't.
- **Hacker-ish mystique:** dim, moody lighting, a gaming headset, low-key delivery. It feels like someone telling you secrets.
- **A side story that humanises him:** "I moved to Japan 2 months ago and joined a soccer team in the countryside… I think this is my character development arc" (2.9M). Not all tech. The life story pulls just as hard.

**Pattern:** every tech hit is **one surprising "insider" thing, explained simply, that the viewer can feel or use**: their phone can be hacked, their location can be found, their password is weak. Security and privacy are his strongest lane. It's fear plus usefulness.

## 3. Format (very consistent, and it's a brand)
- **Title formula, same every time:** "22yo engineer shares/explains [surprising thing]", white text on a **black band at the top**. You know it's him before the sound comes on.
- **Dark, cinematic look:** dim room, warm low light, a headset mic. Almost no movement.
- **Lowercase serif captions** in the middle ("a hundred meters", "apple cannot patch", "long password"): elegant, not the usual bold caps.
- **Proof cards:** real news headlines stacked over his face (Apple exploit), app logos (1Password, Bitwarden), maps.
- **Comment-keyword CTA:** comments are full of one-word replies ("VPN", "Code", "Vols"), so he says "comment X and I'll send you…". That's the same mechanic you already use.

## 4. Comments
- Mostly the CTA keyword, which inflates the comment count, so don't read it as debate.
- The rest: people sharing tools they use ("not everyone uses the same VPN"), and amazement or fear about the hack.

## 5. What to take for your channel
1. **A fixed title formula is a brand.** His "22yo engineer shares…" works because it's identical every time. Yours could be built on your own credential, e.g. "Software engineer explains…" or "An engineer's take on…" (your words, your choice), always in the same spot.
2. **Security and privacy is a lane you don't have yet,** and it's his biggest: "your phone/location/password is exposed, here's the fix". It's simple for beginners, scary and useful, and it fits your "true vs BS" and "advice" lanes.
3. **"You can do WHAT?" explainers:** one surprising capability (Doppler location tracking, an unpatchable chip), with the news headlines as proof.
4. **Real headlines as proof cards** make a claim feel verified in one second. You already do this; he does it every time.
5. **One life thread now and then** (his Japan arc) does as well as his best tech reels. People follow the person.
